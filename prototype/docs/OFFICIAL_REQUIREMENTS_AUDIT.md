## Items not specified by the supplied PDFs

These are portal or organizer checks and must not be inferred:

- Exact submission cutoff time and timezone on 20 August 2026.
- Accepted file extension(s), file-size limit, and filename convention.
- Whether the prototype URL has a dedicated field or must be embedded in the deck.
- Whether notes, hidden slides, appendices, or external attachments are accepted.
- Final registered team name, member names, registration identifier, and confirmed eligibility.

## Claim boundary

- “3.7 billion people use Unilever products on any given day” appears in the official Case Study, p. 2, but should be cited to that case document if used.
- The Rexona stoppage-time example and the fragmented social-listening/web-scraping problem appear in the official Case Study, p. 3.
- Spoken launch figures such as connected outlets, deployed AI use cases, touchless-order rates, consumer-connect counts, influencer-roster size, and advertisement-cost reduction do not appear in either supplied PDF. They remain transcript claims and require an independently verifiable source or omission.
- Prototype scores, thresholds, costs, operational records, and the 1.2-point outcome are assumptions or synthetic and must remain visibly labeled as such.
