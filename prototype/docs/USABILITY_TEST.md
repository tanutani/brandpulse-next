# Proxy-User Test Protocol

Status: pending five human proxy participants; automated checks are not substituted for this gate.

Give each participant the direct hero link without explanation. Ask them to complete the journey and answer:

1. Why is the initial route constrained?
2. Which brand should own the opportunity, and why?
3. What action changes the route to Test?
4. What was locked before the result?
5. Why was the first asset blocked?
6. Who approved the correction, and what did the synthetic test teach?

Record completion time, wrong turns, whether provenance/counter-evidence were noticed, and whether the participant can explain the weakest gate unaided. Acceptance target: at least four of five participants explain the route correctly and finish within four minutes.

After Rexona, show Surf and the Lifebuoy misinformation row. Ask each participant to explain, in
their own words, why Rexona TESTs, Surf ACTs as a growth activation, and misinformation ACTs as a
defensive response. Acceptance target: at least four of five explain all three distinctions.
